The Humanitarian Challenge

The Gap in Modern Mobility Aids

Canes and guide dogs provide essential contact protection, but cannot recognize approaching hazards, announce directional bearing, or estimate distance before a collision happens.

Traditional Canes

Effective only at arm's reach. Low obstacles or waist-height overhangs (like open car trunks or signs) are frequently missed.

Delayed Perception

By the time a cane taps an unexpected vehicle or pedestrian, reaction windows are often too narrow to avoid disorientation.

The SeeForMe Solution

Continuous computer vision monitors ahead, estimating distance in meters and delivering debounced spatial audio guidance.

Core Capabilities

Built for Real-World Reliability

Every stage of the pipeline is crafted to ensure low latency, minimal false alarms, and clear audio guidance.

YOLOv8 Object Detection

Instant identification of pedestrians, cars, buses, bikes, dogs, chairs, and obstacles at high frame rates.

MiDaS Monocular Depth

Estimates relative depth maps directly from a single RGB camera, eliminating the need for bulky stereo rigs or LiDAR.

Spatial-Depth Fusion

Samples the depth map within each bounding box to determine exact distance brackets and directional bearing.

Intelligent Debouncing

Suppresses repetitive audio chatter. Only repeats alerts after cooldown or when a hazard approaches significantly closer.

1. YOLOv8 Object Detection

Incoming 30 FPS RGB video frames are fed through an optimized YOLOv8 neural network. The detector pinpoints all visible obstacles (pedestrians, vehicles, obstacles, animals), producing bounded coordinate boxes, class classifications, and confidence probabilities in under 25ms.
