Vision & Milestones

Mission, Ethics & Project Roadmap

How SeeForMe was conceived, current developmental progress, and future milestones toward edge device deployment.

Why This Project Exists

Globally, roughly 40 million people are blind and over 250 million experience moderate to severe visual impairment. While tools like guide dogs and white canes provide irreplaceable tactile defense against physical obstacles at ground level, they cannot recognize object categories, read contextual surroundings, or estimate distances in advance.

SeeForMe prototypes the core software pipeline to bridge that sensory gap — acting like a calm, reliable, sighted companion delivering real-time verbal alerts.

Project Development Roadmap

STAGE 1 • DONE
Backend Prototype

YOLOv8n object detection + MiDaS depth running on camera feeds via FastAPI server.

STAGE 2 • DONE
Text-to-Speech (TTS) Audio Generation

Integrated browser Web Speech API vocalizer with low-latency spoken guidance.

STAGE 3 • DONE
Prioritization & Debouncing Logic

Central walking-path filter, distance-drop triggers, and cooldown timers to prevent repetitive chatter.

STAGE 4 • DONE
Modern Interactive Multi-Page Web Platform

Production-grade landing page, dedicated live vision dashboard, photo test lab, and developer documentation.

STAGE 5 • IN PROGRESS
Latency & Frame-Budget Benchmarking

Optimizing pipeline cycle times to achieve consistent 300–500ms response on consumer hardware.

STAGE 6 • PLANNED
On-Device Mobile Deployment (TFLite / CoreML)

Quantize models for offline inference directly on iOS (Neural Engine) and Android (NNAPI).

STAGE 7 • STRETCH
Fine-Tuning on Walkway Hazards

Train custom classes for curbs, stairs, drop-offs, and potholes using Mapillary and Roboflow Universe.

STAGE 8 • STRETCH
User Studies & Assistive Testing

Pilot field trials with visually impaired participants to evaluate auditory ergonomics and cognitive load.

Safety & Ethical Disclaimer

This software is developed strictly for educational, experimental, and portfolio demonstration purposes. It has not been evaluated or certified by medical, assistive technology, or safety regulatory authorities.

Do not rely on SeeForMe as a safety-critical navigation aid, and never use it in hazardous traffic conditions without traditional mobility training and certified aids (such as a white cane or guide dog).
